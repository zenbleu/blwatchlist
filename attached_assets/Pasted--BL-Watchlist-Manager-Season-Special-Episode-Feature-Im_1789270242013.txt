# BL Watchlist Manager — Season + Special Episode Feature

Implement two additions to the existing BL Watchlist Manager: **Season Support** and **Special Episodes in the Release Calendar**.

**Preserve all existing functionality and UI. Do not rebuild or modify unrelated features.**

### 1. Season Support

Add an optional **Season** field to BL Series & Movies entries:

* Standalone
* Season 1
* Season 2
* Season 3, etc.
* Allow higher/custom season numbers if needed.

A season must remain an **independent entry** with its own:

* Poster
* Release year
* Status
* Favorite/evaluation
* Episode progress
* Airing schedule
* Top 10 eligibility/ranking

Example:

> KinnPorsche — Season 1 — 2022 — Complete
> KinnPorsche — Season 2 — 2026 — Ongoing

Do **not** include "Season 1" inside the actual title name. Store/display it as separate metadata.

Prevent accidental duplicate season entries for the same title/season.

Season information must appear naturally on entry cards and in relevant detail/edit views.

### 2. Special Episodes

Special episodes must **NOT be separate BL Watchlist entries**.

They belong to their corresponding **season** and should be managed inside the existing **Release Calendar**.

Add a separate section:

**Regular Episodes**

* Existing episode calendar/progress remains unchanged.

**Special Episodes**

* `+ Add Special`
* Special number
* Special title
* Release date
* Optional release time
* Released/upcoming status

Example:

> ✨ Special 1 — Behind the Scenes — June 28
> ✨ Special 2 — Director's Cut — July 5

Special episodes should **not affect the main `Watched X / Total Episodes` counter**. They should have their own watched/released state within the Release Calendar.

### 3. Schedule & Countdown Compatibility

Regular episode scheduling continues using the existing:

**Air Days + Airing Time + Countdown**

Do not replace or interfere with the existing recurring airing schedule.

Special Episodes may have their own release date/time in the Release Calendar, but they must **not override the main airing schedule or countdown**.

### 4. Important Preservation Rules

* Existing entries must remain valid.
* Existing JSON import/export must remain compatible.
* Existing Favorites, Top 10, Ongoing BL, Statistics, Overview, and Settings must continue working.
* A season counts as **one entry**, just like any existing BL entry.
* Do not create a new tab for Seasons or Special Episodes.
* Keep the design responsive on **desktop**.

**Only implement the Season and Special Episode features. Do not touch unrelated working components.**
