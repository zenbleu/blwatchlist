Rating System: Major Adjustment

[Revised] Core rating categories:

Storyline - The narrative focus, plot depth, or content intensity assigned to a story's plot.
Acting - Actors' lead performance, technical skill, emotional range, and ability to fully embody a character.
Chemistry - The perceived connection, energetic resonance, and mutual responsiveness between two characters.
Music - How well the music supports the story, builds emotion, and defines the show's atmosphere.
Production - The technical, auditory, and visual crafts seamlessly work together to construct the project.

[Addition] Add a symbol [ⓘ] beside each category where when the computer cursor hovers this symbol, it displays the description of a category.

IMPORTANT: Retain original UI star rating design.

[Revised] Bonus Evaluation Categories:

Originality: Broke away from standard and cliché tropes.
Character Depth: The characters had genuine flaws, realistic motives, and grew significantly by the final episode.
Relationship Dynamics: The recurring pattern of behaviors, communication, and emotional interactions that take place between two characters.
Outstanding Chemistry: The actors had an electric connection that felt completely natural and elevated every scene they shared in or off-cam.
Natural Skinship: The physical affection (hugs, kisses, comfort) felt authentic and emotionally resonant, not awkward or stiff.
Secondary Couple (Side CP) or Supporting Characters: The supporting characters/couple were genuinely engaging and didn't feel like filler material to stretch the runtime.
Soundtrack (OST): The BGM and theme songs perfectly enhanced the emotional beats and stayed stuck in the head long after.
Cinematography: Gorgeous color grading, beautiful lighting, and creative camera work that made the show feel premium.
Emotional Impact: How intensely the show moves, saddens, excites, or engages the audience beyond its technical quality.
Ending: No sudden open-ended trauma, rushed time-skips, or forced tragedies; the story stuck the landing.
Comfort Aura: The show provides an elite, stress-free atmosphere that makes it a perfect escape to watch on.
Rewatch Value: How enjoyable, rewarding, or necessary it is to watch multiple times.

We will change how the 5 core rating categories and bonus evaluation will affect the overall rating of an entry which is fixed at 10.

We will use a hybrid approach: use the new episode tracker data to directly calculate one specific core category, while keeping the other four strictly subjective.

🎬 Storyline (Calculated): This should be the direct average of all episode ratings. Plot and Pacing are inherently structural. If individual episodes dragged or felt like filler, episode scores will naturally reflect that, creating a highly accurate mathematical representation of the show's overall storytelling flow.
👩‍❤️‍👨 Chemistry and Acting (Manual): Keep this manual. A show might have a terrible, messy plot (low episode scores), but the actors' raw chemistry might be completely off the charts. The user should be able to give this a 10/10 regardless of what happened in individual episodes.
🎨 Production and Music (Manual): Keep this manual. Color grading, soundtracks, and set designs are usually consistent throughout the series. There's no need to calculate this from individual episodes.

The "Crown / Hall of Fame" Visual Visualizer
