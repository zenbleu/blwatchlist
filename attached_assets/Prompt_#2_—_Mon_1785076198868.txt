Prompt #2 — Monthly BL Wrapped (Frontend / User Experience)

> Implement the complete user experience for the Monthly BL Wrapped feature using the backend system that has already been implemented.

This prompt is ONLY for the frontend, presentation, animations, transitions, and user experience. Do NOT redesign or modify the backend tracking logic from Prompt #1.


---

Goal

Monthly BL Wrapped is a monthly celebration of the user's BL journey.

It should feel warm, polished, rewarding, and memorable.

The experience is inspired by Spotify Wrapped, but it must maintain the unique identity of the BL Watchlist app.

It should never feel like a report or dashboard. Instead, it should feel like flipping through a beautiful storybook that reflects the user's BL month.


---

When It Appears

If a Monthly Wrapped has not yet been viewed by the user:

Automatically present it the first time the app is opened after that month has ended.

The user may skip it.

After skipping or finishing it, it must always remain accessible from a dedicated "Wrapped History" section.


Do not repeatedly force the user to watch it after it has already been viewed.


---

Presentation Style

The experience should be:

Full-screen.

Mobile-first.

Smooth and immersive.

Premium feeling.

Easy to navigate.

Optimized for touch controls.


Every page should transition naturally into the next using elegant animations inspired by Spotify Wrapped while matching the existing BL Watchlist visual identity.

Avoid clutter.

Every screen should focus on one story or one statistic at a time.


---

Navigation

Users should be able to:

Tap to continue.

Swipe between pages.

Go back to previous pages.

Pause and resume anytime.

Exit the Wrapped.

Replay it later from Wrapped History.



---

Monthly Wrapped Content

Display only statistics that actually occurred during that month (expect Planned if it was on watchlist for so long)

If a category has no activity, it should not appear.

Examples include (but are not limited to):

Monthly introduction

Total BL entries added

Completed titles

Ongoing BL

Planned BL

Favorite additions

Ratings given

Highest-rated BL

Average monthly rating

Most watched country

Collection growth

Top 10 updates

Achievement unlocks

Rank progression

New personal records

Other meaningful monthly highlights


The order should feel like a story instead of a statistics report.


---

Quiet Months

If the month contains no BL activity:

Generate a simplified Monthly Wrapped experience instead of skipping it.

Keep it short and reflective.

The experience should acknowledge that the month was part of the user's BL journey without presenting empty statistics.

(Do not hardcode final wording; appropriate copy can be created during implementation.)


---

Ending

Every Monthly Wrapped should conclude with a warm ending that celebrates the completed month and naturally welcomes the user's next BL journey.

The ending should feel satisfying regardless of whether the month was very active or completely quiet.


---

Wrapped History

Create a dedicated Wrapped History section where users can revisit every Monthly Wrapped they have unlocked.

History should:

Display all months chronologically.

Preserve every Wrapped permanently.

Allow replaying any previous month at any time.

Clearly distinguish viewed and unviewed Wrapped summaries.



---

Performance

Animations should remain smooth on lower-end Android devices.

Avoid unnecessary heavy rendering or excessive animation complexity.

Prioritize fluidity and responsiveness.


---

Future Compatibility

Design the Monthly Wrapped presentation system so it can be reused for the future Annual BL Wrapped feature.

Reuse common animation systems, navigation, transitions, and presentation architecture whenever possible while allowing Annual Wrapped to expand into a larger and more cinematic experience.

The Monthly Wrapped should establish the visual language and interaction style that the Annual Wrapped will later build upon.
