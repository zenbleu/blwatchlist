name: Episode/storyline rating priority
description: User-confirmed precedence between a completed entry's final storyline score and its episode-rating average.
---

**Rule:** For completed entries, use the saved final-storyline score while the final-storyline slide is available (that is, until all episodes in the saved total are rated). Once every episode is rated, use the episode average. Ongoing entries keep their existing episode-average behavior.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose the final-storyline score as the active rating until full episode-level coverage is complete.

**How to apply:** Keep slide visibility and overall-rating calculation aligned: a completed entry with any unrated episode can use its final-storyline score; rating the last missing episode switches the effective Storyline category to the episode average.
